# Credit-Card-Fraud-Detection-Analysis
This dataset contains 100,000 real-world-style credit card transactions spanning the full calendar year 2024, with 43 features covering transaction details, customer profiles, velocity signals, risk indicators, device and channel information, and account behaviour. Every distribution, fraud rate, and risk signal relationship is calibrated against published industry research from the Federal Reserve, Nilson Report, FICO, and the European Central Bank.

## Problem Statement
Fraud accounts for about 2% of transactions but causes significant financial loss. This project analyzes 100,000 credit card transactions to identify the hours, channels, and behaviors most associated with
fraud, so monitoring can be focused where risk is highest.

## Business Questions
1. What is the overall fraud rate and total loss?
2. Which hours of the day carry the highest fraud risk?
3. Which merchant categories and channels are most affected?
4. Which fraud types cause the largest losses?
5. Which transaction signals distinguish fraud from legitimate activity?
